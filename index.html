Coverage Eligibility

Firelight enables coverage for the major technical and economic failure modes of on-chain protocols:

Smart Contract Exploits

Bugs or vulnerabilities in a covered protocol's code, such as logic errors, reentrancy, access control failures, upgrade flaws.

Oracle Failures

Incorrect price data related to misconfigured or stale feeds, compromised sources, or price manipulation.

Governance Exploits

Hijacked governance such as flash-loan voting attacks, malicious upgrades, exploited governance contracts.

Bad Debt from Mechanism Failure

Liquidation or collateral mechanisms malfunction, socializing unrecoverable bad debt to depositors.

Depegs from Mechanism Failure

A pegged asset breaks from its reference value because its stabilization mechanism fails.

Redemption Failures

A protocol defect blocks withdrawals or returns materially less than fair value.
